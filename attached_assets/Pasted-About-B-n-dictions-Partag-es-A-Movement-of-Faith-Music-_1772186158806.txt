About Bénédictions Partagées
A Movement of Faith, Music, and Unity

Bénédictions Partagées (BP) was born from a simple but powerful vision:
to create a digital space where faith is strengthened, worship is alive, and believers are united.

In a world filled with noise, distraction, and uncertainty, we believe people are searching for something deeper — something eternal. BP exists to offer that connection through gospel music, daily encouragement, and the power of prayer.

Our Mission

Our mission is to:

Spread the Gospel through music and media

Encourage believers with daily devotionals

Create a safe space for prayer and testimonies

Unite churches and communities through digital ministry

We believe technology is not just a tool for entertainment — it can be a vessel for transformation.

Why We Created This App

Bénédictions Partagées was created to bring worship and encouragement directly into your daily life.

Whether you are:

Driving to work

Starting your morning devotion

Going through a difficult season

Or celebrating a breakthrough

BP is designed to walk with you.

This is more than a streaming app.
It is a spiritual companion.

What You’ll Find Inside
Gospel Music with Purpose

From Afrobeat to Kompa, worship to contemporary gospel — every song points to Jesus Christ and carries a message of hope and redemption.

Daily Verse & Encouragement

A word for your day. A reminder that God is present, faithful, and working.

Prayer Requests & Community Support

We believe in the power of collective prayer. Here, you can share your burden and know you are not alone.

Testimonies

Because when God moves, His works must be shared.

Our Core Values

Faith – Christ-centered in everything we do.
Integrity – Honest, transparent, and responsible.
Unity – Bridging churches and believers together.
Excellence – Offering high-quality content that honors God.
